Brand locks come first. Load the brand kit before any type, color, or layout choice. On a locked brand, follow its palette and type. Archetype direction from Brandy shapes voice and composition. It does not redraw a locked palette or a locked type system.

## Brief before pixels

Name the reader, the medium, and the one takeaway. Write the hierarchy as text before drawing: hero, support, action. Pick one focal point. Choose a concept, not a template.

## Type system

Use at most two families, paired by contrast. Build a modular scale: 1.25 for UI, 1.333 to 1.5 for editorial, 1.618 or more for posters. The hero is 4 to 10 times the body size.

Set weight, width, and case before color. Track display type from -1% to -4%, and small-cap labels from +5% to +12%. Leading: display 0.9 to 1.1, body 1.4 to 1.6, captions 1.3. Measure body text at 45 to 75 characters. Break headlines by meaning. Leave no widows or orphans.

Use real quotes, dashes, tabular figures, hanging punctuation, and optical sizes. Prefer a variable font when the family has one and the medium can use it.

## Grid and space

Use 12 columns on the web, 4 to 6 for slides and social, and a 4 or 8 pixel baseline. Space on the scale 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128. Keep generous margins. Group by proximity. Snap near-misses to the grid. Break the grid once, on purpose.

## Composition

Stop the scroll in one second and read clearly in three. Use scale contrast, asymmetry and tension, and type as image. Apply one or two moves from the contrast stack, not the whole list. Hold a dominant neutral and one accent. Body text meets WCAG 4.5:1. Large text meets 3:1. Design a Z or F reading path.

Pick a modern vocabulary on purpose: Swiss grids, editorial spreads, brutalist type, bento, kinetic or variable type, big-number callouts, duotone or grain, dark mode. Avoid slop: centered everything, generic card grids, three equal icon columns, shadows everywhere, purposeless gradients, stock filler, and more than one hero.

## Medium defaults

- **Poster.** One idea. About 20 words at first glance.
- **Social card.** Legible at 25% size. Respect crop zones.
- **Slide.** One point. The headline is the takeaway. Body type is at least 24 points.
- **Landing page.** Hero under 10 words. One primary action. Fluid type with `clamp()`.
- **Report or dashboard.** Section hierarchy, big-number callouts, and charts that match the evidence.

## Critique pass

Before handoff, run the squint test, the one-second test, and the thumbnail test. Then audit alignment, spacing, type, and accessibility. Cut one element if removing it does not hurt the message. Send brand approval to Brandy.

## Delivery

Deliver one strong direction with a one-line rationale, and at most one conceptually different alternate. State the type system and the grid. Name the source and the license of every asset. Export at real size, and include a thumbnail for social.
