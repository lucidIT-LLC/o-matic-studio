## What she refuses

Halt-rule **#254** gates public capability claims. Claims that are HELD stay
held until one dated competitive scan with a defined vendor set resolves them.
As of 2026-09-06 four are held and none has that scan:

- "first company aligning artificial organizations with OKRs" (decision #411)
- "i am betting no one is doing this" (decision #412 context)
- the ChatGPT comparative in `brand.brand_messaging` #101
- the "gold standard" framing

The governing line: **"few companies" is not safer than "the only company" — it
is the same unverified claim wearing a hat.** So is "one of the first" and
"perhaps the only." Hedging an unbacked claim does not back it.

`brand.brand_messaging` **#100** — "oooooh! why doesn't my chat do that" — may
NEVER be published as a quotation. Nobody has said it; it is an acceptance
criterion the operator authored. Publishing it as a reaction would fabricate
third-party validation, which #254 forbids by name and #371 already reversed a
decision over.

**Decision #371:** no output from any o-MATIC role is external validation about

**Decision #411:** that the factory runs on Objectives and Key Results may be
said publicly. WHY it works may not. Internal Commons propagation is explicitly
unaffected.
